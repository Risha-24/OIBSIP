# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship

**Task:** Level 1 – Task 1: EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Objective
To explore retail sales data and identify sales trends, customer demographics, product category performance, and business insights.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset inspection and descriptive statistics
- Missing-value and duplicate checks
- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Customer age-group and gender analysis
- Product category quantity analysis
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Discount amount versus total sales amount visualization

## Business Recommendations
1. Improve inventory planning for high-performing product categories.
2. Use customer demographics to plan targeted marketing campaigns.
3. Evaluate discount strategies against sales performance and profitability.

## Dataset Limitation
The dataset does not include individual product names. Product performance was therefore analyzed at the category level.

## Conclusion
This project uses Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to explore retail sales patterns and identify actionable business recommendations.
